Privacy Policy — Dirt 2 Dream Builds

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

Dirt 2 Dream Builds ("we", "us") is a content creator brand that publishes construction timelapse videos of dream homes on social media platforms, including TikTok.

2. What this app does

Our application uses the TikTok Content Posting API to publish videos that we create to our own TikTok account (@dirt2dreambuilds). It does not collect, access, or process any data from TikTok users who view our content.

3. Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data from viewers of our content. Our use of the TikTok API is limited to:

  • Publishing videos to our own TikTok account
  • Reading basic metrics (views, likes) of our own published videos

4. Data we store

We store only the OAuth authentication tokens required to publish to our own account, along with our own video files. These are kept on a private, secured server and are never shared with third parties.

5. Third-party services

We use the TikTok Content Posting API under TikTok's own terms. Any data processed by TikTok when you interact with our content is governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy.

6. Contact

For any questions about this policy, contact us at: dirt2dreambuilds@gmail.com
